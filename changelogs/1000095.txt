First complete Android, desktop and optional self-hosted sync release.

Organize podcasts and YouTube channels into groups with their own feeds.
Stream or download audio, manage Up next, chapters and playback settings,
and import or export subscriptions and encrypted backups.

Desktop packages include their Java runtime and audio and YouTube engines.
The optional sync server keeps subscriptions and listening state in step;
it never stores podcast audio or feed contents.

App updates link to GitHub Releases for manual installation. YouTube engine
updates follow the approved-engine policy.
