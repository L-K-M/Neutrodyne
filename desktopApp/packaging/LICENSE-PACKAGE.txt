Neutrodyne desktop package: licences
====================================

Neutrodyne's own code is released into the public domain under the
Unlicense; its text follows at the end of this file. This package also
bundles third-party components, which keep their own licences:

- Java and Kotlin libraries: Compose Multiplatform, the AndroidX
  libraries with their bundled SQLite (public domain), Ktor, OkHttp,
  Okio, Coil, kotlinx, Metro, JNA (used under Apache-2.0), jsoup, kxml2
  and others, under Apache-2.0, MIT and BSD-3-Clause.
- Skiko's native library: Skia (BSD-3-Clause) with FreeType (FTL, Linux
  only), libpng (libpng-2.0), libjpeg-turbo (IJG, BSD-3-Clause and
  Zlib), HarfBuzz (MIT-Modern-Variant), ICU (Unicode-3.0), libwebp
  (BSD-3-Clause), expat (MIT), zlib (Zlib), Wuffs (Apache-2.0) and, on
  Linux, the Vulkan Memory Allocator (MIT).
- OkHttp's copy of the Public Suffix List: unmodified data under
  MPL-2.0 (source: https://publicsuffix.org/list/public_suffix_list.dat).
- The OpenJDK runtime (Eclipse Temurin), unmodified: GPL-2.0 with the
  Classpath Exception. Its notices are in the runtime's legal directory
  inside the installation (lib/runtime/legal on Linux, runtime\legal on
  Windows, Contents/runtime/Contents/Home/legal on macOS). Its exact
  source is attached to the release as
  openjdk-<version>-temurin-sources.tar.gz.

Every bundled component is listed with its full licence text under
Settings > Licences in the app. Source code:
